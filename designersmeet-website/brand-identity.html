Our Process

Five stages to a brand
built to endure.

01
Stage One
Brand Audit
We examine your current brand position (if any), your competitors, your target audience, and where you want to go. This shapes every creative decision that follows.
02
Stage Two
Strategy Session
A deep 90-minute session where we define your brand's purpose, personality, positioning, and promise. The creative brief is locked here.
03
Stage Three
Concept Development
We develop 2-3 distinct creative directions, each with a full rationale. You see real concepts — not just mood boards — grounded in your strategy.
04
Stage Four
Refinement
We take the chosen direction and refine it across every touchpoint — logomark, wordmark, colour, type, pattern. Two rounds of revisions are included.
05
Stage Five
Brand Book Delivery
You receive a comprehensive brand guidelines document, all source files, and a 30-minute handover call. Your brand is ready to deploy everywhere.
