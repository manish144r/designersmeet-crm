How We Work

A Process Built on Evidence.

Every design decision is backed by research, validated by users, and documented for your team.

01
Research
User interviews, surveys, analytics deep-dives, and competitive analysis to understand the real problem.
02
Architecture
Defining the information structure, user flows, and site maps that serve your users' mental models.
03
Wireframes
Low-fidelity layouts focused purely on function — structure over aesthetics, tested early and often.
04
Prototype
Interactive Figma prototype connected to real user flows, ready for moderated usability testing.
05
Handoff
Developer-ready specs, exported assets, and a living design system your team can maintain.
